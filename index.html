Built for the remote

Made for the big screen.

Big artwork, clear focus and rows that glide with your remote. Everything is a click or two away, from the couch.

  • Build your own home: Continue Watching, Next Up, Recently Added, Because You Watched, genres, collections and more, in any order.
  • One menu for it all: search, favorites, genres and every library.
  • Sign in with Quick Connect: no typing passwords on the TV.
  • A screensaver that fits: your library's artwork, a poster wall, a clock or a floating logo.

New Trailer previews

Trailers, right where you're looking.

Rest on a movie or show for a moment and its trailer starts playing right in the card. Posters widen to fit it, and the featured banner plays one too.

  • Your server's trailers first, then the title's trailer on YouTube.
  • Your call: turn previews off, choose the delay, or play them with sound.
  • A Trailer button on every movie and show page, too.

Customization

Make it yours.

Chameleon changes to suit you. Pick a theme, or build your own from any two colors, then choose the fonts and icons to match.

  • Eight themes, or a custom one from any two colors.
  • Any Google Font for headings, text and subtitles.
  • Five icon styles, and posters or thumbnails for every section.
  • Profiles with their own theme, fonts and playback settings.

Playback

Picture and sound, untouched.

Chameleon plays what your server has the way it was made: the best picture your TV can show, and surround sound sent to your receiver bit for bit.

High dynamic range

HDR plays as HDR, and Dolby Vision as Dolby Vision on devices that support it, with a second player engine standing by for anything the first can't handle.

Dolby VisionHDR10+HDR10HLG

Passthrough audio

Lossless and surround tracks go straight to your receiver or soundbar untouched, and decoded surround stays surround.
